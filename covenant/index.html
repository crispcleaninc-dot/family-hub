Family Covenant

Our family's covenant app, with everything from Community Covenant inside: Wisdom Reels (including the founder's voice), skits, gospel music, Cover to Cover, studies, and sharing by text.

Play in the background

Start Wisdom Reels or the music in Reels & Music, then lock your phone. The reel or song shows on your lock screen and notifications with play, pause, next and back. Reels keep reading with the screen off. Gospel songs come from YouTube, which may pause them when the screen locks.

On the TV or a speaker

Tap the 📺 TV button at the top of Wisdom Reels to send it to a Chromecast/Google TV or Apple TV. Bluetooth speakers and cars just work once connected in your phone's settings.

Install it

  • Android (Chrome): tap Install below, or menu ⋮ › Add to Home screen / Install app.
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share ⎋ › Add to Home Screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): the install icon in the address bar, or the Install button below.
