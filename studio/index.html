Install Voice & Avatar Studio

iPhone / iPad
Open this page in Safari → tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android
Open in Chrome → tap ⋮ → Install app.

Windows / Mac
Open in Chrome or Edge → click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

After that it opens from its own icon like any app, and your recordings still send by themselves.

Sign in with your Profit Engine PIN

Pick what to read, tap the red button, and read. When you tap Stop, the recording saves itself to your private Drive folder. Nothing to attach.

My files

Everything here is private to you, in Drive: "Voice & Avatar Studio (private)". Used only for your own videos and your avatar.

Looks

Your avatar's closet. Add one clear front-facing photo in My files first. Pick the looks you want, and they're made from your photo.
