Opening your Hub…

Open in the browser instead

You're offline. The Hub needs internet to show your cameras and alerts. It will open as soon as you're connected.

iPhone / iPad: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
Windows / Mac: in Chrome or Edge click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

After installing, open it from the new “Home Hub” icon. It opens your Hub sign-in inside the app.
