📖 Story problem (word problem)

Read the whole story problem out loud, or type it. I'll find the numbers, tell you what kind of problem it is, and set it up with you.

Talk with me: tap once, then just talk like you would to a tutor. It listens after it finishes speaking. Say "I wrote it" to go on, say your answers out loud ("twelve"), or say "slow down", "spell it", "why", "say that again", "new problem", or "stop listening". Questions to try: "What is 9867 divided by 12?", "How many times does 53 go into 158?", "Why do we subtract?", "Next", "Say that again", "What's the answer?" During "Let me try" he can just say his answer.

How I learn best

👪 Parent controls

Once a child shows they've mastered a skill (3 problems solved on their own, with 85% or more right on the first try), "Show me how," the answers and step answers lock automatically for that skill. Unlock them here if they need more help.

Install this app on a phone or computer

iPhone/iPad (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
Computer (Chrome/Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
